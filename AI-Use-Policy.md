# CMP 131 – Artificial Intelligence Use Policy

AI tools, including GitHub Copilot and ChatGPT, may be used as learning assistants in this course. They must support your learning rather than replace your own thinking and programming.

You may use AI to explain Python concepts and errors, provide small hints, help troubleshoot code you already wrote, suggest general tests, and improve comments or variable names.

You may not use AI to generate a complete or partial graded solution, replace an entire program or function, submit code you cannot explain, or misrepresent AI-generated work as your own.

You are responsible for every line of code you submit and must be able to explain and test your work.

If you use an AI tool, complete `AI-Use-Report.md`. If you do not use AI, indicate that in the report.

AI assistance must follow the course academic-integrity policy. When uncertain whether a particular use is allowed, ask the instructor.
